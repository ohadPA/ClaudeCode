**Status:** For Leadership Review

## Investment Required

**$100,000** (4 eng-months, fully loaded)

## Pessimistic Scenario (Things Go Poorly)

**Assumptions:**
- Only 30% of new signups see guided onboarding (slow rollout, technical issues)
- Modest lift: 45% → 50% activation (+5pp) — sample tasks help some users but aren't compelling enough for most
- No measurable retention improvement

**Impact:**
- Users exposed: 5,000 × 30% = 1,500/month
- Current activated: 1,500 × 45% = 675/month
- Projected activated: 1,500 × 50% = 750/month
- **Incremental: +75 users/month**
- Revenue: 75 × $12 × 60% = $540 MRR = **$6,480 ARR**
- 3-year LTV: 75 × 12 × $172.80 = **$155,520**
- **3-year ROI: 1.6x**

**What this means:** Even if things go poorly, we still get a positive return on investment over 3 years. We don't lose money.

---

## Realistic Scenario (Expected Case)

**Assumptions:**
- 70% of new signups see guided onboarding (standard rollout to all new users)
- Strong lift: 45% → 58% activation (+13pp) — addresses the primary confusion identified in survey data
- Modest retention improvement (not modeled in numbers)

**Impact:**
- Users exposed: 5,000 × 70% = 3,500/month
- Current activated: 3,500 × 45% = 1,575/month
- Projected activated: 3,500 × 58% = 2,030/month
- **Incremental: +455 users/month**
- Revenue: 455 × $12 × 60% = $3,276 MRR = **$39,312 ARR**
- 3-year LTV: 455 × 12 × $172.80 = **$943,296**
- **3-year ROI: 9.4x**

**What this means:** This is our most likely outcome based on data. Strong ROI that compounds over time as each month's cohort adds incremental LTV.

---

## Optimistic Scenario (Things Go Great)

**Assumptions:**
- 90% of new signups see guided onboarding (fast rollout, high visibility)
- Strong lift: 45% → 62% activation (+17pp) — sample project resonates deeply, users engage beyond just activation
- Retention improvement: activated users stay 2.5x longer (historical correlation)
- Template adoption drives ongoing engagement

**Impact:**
- Users exposed: 5,000 × 90% = 4,500/month
- Current activated: 4,500 × 45% = 2,025/month
- Projected activated: 4,500 × 62% = 2,790/month
- **Incremental: +765 users/month**
- Revenue: 765 × $12 × 60% = $5,508 MRR = **$66,096 ARR**
- 3-year LTV: 765 × 12 × $172.80 = **$1,586,304**
- **3-year ROI: 15.9x**

**What this means:** If the feature resonates as strongly as the survey data suggests, the upside is massive. Plus, the retention multiplier means actual LTV is even higher than modeled.

---

## Retention Multiplier (Not Modeled Above)

Historical data shows that activated users stay **2.5x longer** than non-activated users:
- Non-activated average lifetime: 3 months
- Activated average lifetime: 24 months

This means every incremental activation has a compounding effect on LTV that our models above **understate**. The true 3-year value is likely 30-50% higher than projected when accounting for improved retention.
