## 3. Notion Document

# User Research Synthesis: 8 Interviews, 5 Key Themes

**TL;DR:** We interviewed 8 users across 7 companies and every major persona. Five pain points emerged with overwhelming consistency: notification overload (100%), dark mode (100%), template library (88%), mobile experience (88%), and confusing onboarding (63%). These findings validate our Q1 roadmap priorities and provide clear direction for product improvements.

## Background

Between October 5-13, 2024, we conducted 8 user interviews to understand product pain points and validate our Q1 priorities. Participants represented our full persona spectrum:

- **Enterprise Admin** — Rachel Kim, Director of IT Ops, FinanceTech (650 employees)
- **IC Engineer** — David Chen, Senior Backend Engineer, GrowthLabs (180 employees)
- **Engineering Manager** — Lisa Patel, DataFlow (220 employees)
- **Product Designer** — Maya Rodriguez, CloudSync (95 employees)
- **Customer Success** — James Park, TaskFlow internal (45 users)
- **Marketing Manager** — Sarah Thompson, DevTools Inc (140 employees)
- **Sales Operations** — Marcus Williams, Enterprise SaaS Co (280 employees)
- **Junior PM** — Priya Singh, FinTech Startup (65 employees)

## Key Findings

### 1. Notification Overload (8/8 users)
Every user independently raised notifications as a major pain point. Daily notification volumes range from 30 to 70+, with most users resorting to turning notifications off entirely — then missing critical updates.

**What users want:**
- Tiered system (urgent / important / FYI)
- Daily digest mode for non-urgent items
- Timezone-aware delivery (no 2am pings)
- Per-project notification controls

**Supporting quotes:**
- *"I get emails for everything. Someone breathes near a task I'm watching, I get an email."* — David (Engineer)
- *"60-70 notifications a day. I've had to turn most off."* — Marcus (Sales Ops)
- *"Alert me immediately if a task is tagged urgent, batch everything else."* — James (CS Manager)

### 2. Dark Mode (8/8 users)
Universally requested across every role, company size, and persona. Teams work across timezones, late-night sessions are common, and the bright interface causes real discomfort.

**Supporting quotes:**
- *"No dark mode! I work late, bright white interface hurts my eyes."* — David (Engineer)
- *"It's a running joke on our team Slack."* — Sarah T. (Marketing)

### 3. Template Library (7/8 users)
Users are spending significant time manually recreating identical project structures. This affects both new user activation (blank screen problem) and existing user efficiency.

**Use cases identified:**
- Customer onboarding checklists (CS)
- Campaign playbooks (Marketing)
- Sales rep onboarding (Sales Ops)
- Sprint planning structures (Engineering)
- Design project workflows (Design)
- New hire onboarding (Managers)

**Time savings estimate:** 2-5 hours/month per active user

### 4. Mobile Experience (7/8 users)
The mobile web version is consistently described as clunky. Users who travel or work on-the-go are delaying updates until they're back at a laptop.

**Common complaints:**
- Excessive scrolling
- Difficult to update tasks
- Poor attachment/image viewing
- Slow performance

### 5. Blank Screen Onboarding (5/8 users)
New users feel overwhelmed by the empty workspace. Current time-to-first-task is 45 minutes vs. competitor average of 15 minutes. This is a primary driver of our 45% activation rate.
