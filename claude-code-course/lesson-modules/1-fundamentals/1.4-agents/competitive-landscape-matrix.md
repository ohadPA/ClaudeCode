## Key Opportunities for TaskFlow

1. **Async-first is our moat** — No competitor is built specifically for remote/async teams. This is TaskFlow's unique positioning that none can easily replicate.

2. **Simplicity gap** — Jira is too complex, ClickUp is too bloated, Asana is too expensive, Monday.com is too cluttered. TaskFlow sits in the sweet spot: powerful enough but not overwhelming.

3. **Performance advantage** — TaskFlow and Linear are the speed leaders. ClickUp, Monday.com, and Asana all have performance complaints. Speed is a retention driver.

4. **Cross-functional teams** — Linear is engineering-only, Jira is developer-heavy. TaskFlow serves PMs, designers, marketers, AND engineers. This is a real differentiator.

## Key Threats

1. **AI features** — Asana, Linear, Monday.com, and ClickUp are all shipping AI. TaskFlow needs an AI roadmap for 2025.
2. **Brand awareness** — Monday.com outspends us 10:1 on marketing. Mind-share is a real battle.
3. **Linear expanding** — Linear is moving beyond engineering teams into our territory.
4. **Enterprise table stakes** — SSO, audit logs, and compliance are must-haves we're still building.

## Strategic Recommendation

**Double down on:** Async-first positioning, performance, simplicity, cross-functional teams
**Invest in:** Mobile app, enterprise features, AI capabilities, templates
**Compete on:** Price (vs Asana), quality (vs ClickUp), simplicity (vs Jira), breadth (vs Linear)
