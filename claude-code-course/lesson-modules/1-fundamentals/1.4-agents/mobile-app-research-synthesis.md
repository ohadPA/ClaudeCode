# Mobile App Research Synthesis

*Compiled from 4 specialized agent analyses: Interviews, Survey, Support Tickets, Sales Notes*

---

## Executive Summary

The data is overwhelming and unanimous: **TaskFlow must ship a native mobile app.** Every data source points to the same conclusion with zero dissent.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Universal Demand (Interviews)
- **100% of interviewed users** cited mobile as a pain point
- Top issues: slow mobile web, no offline access, no native app
- Users are finding desperate workarounds (screenshotting boards, buying iPads)

### 2. Quantitative Validation (Survey)
- **99% of 200 survey respondents** want a native mobile app
- Current mobile web satisfaction: **1.5 out of 3** (very poor)
- 86% rate mobile as "critical" or "high" importance
- Demand is equal across ALL roles — not just field workers

### 3. Churn Risk (Support Tickets)
- **$420K ARR at risk** from enterprise renewals contingent on mobile
- 10/10 support tickets analyzed were mobile-related
- Healthcare, manufacturing, and construction verticals at highest risk
- Security/compliance requirements (HIPAA, MDM) need mobile-native solutions

### 4. Revenue Impact (Sales)
- **5 confirmed lost deals** due to no mobile app
- **$847K ARR at risk** across 12 impacted deals
- **$287K win-back opportunity** if mobile ships
- Competitors cited: Asana (4 deals), Monday.com (3), Linear (2)
- Mobile is a primary disqualifier in 30%+ of deals

---

## Recommendation

**Ship native mobile app as highest priority (Q1 2025).**

The business case is clear:
- Protect $420K in at-risk renewals
- Unlock $287K in win-back revenue
- Stop losing 30%+ of deals to competitors
- Address the #1 user pain point across all personas

---

*Sources: 5 user interviews, 200 survey responses, 10 support tickets, sales pipeline analysis*
