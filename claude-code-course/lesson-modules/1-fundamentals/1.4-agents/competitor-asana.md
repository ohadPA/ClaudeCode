# Competitor Report: Asana

## Overview
Asana is an enterprise-grade project management and work management platform founded in 2008. It serves teams of all sizes with tools for task tracking, project planning, and workflow automation.

## Key Features
- Multi-view project management (list, board, timeline, calendar)
- Advanced dependency tracking and critical path management
- Portfolio management and resource allocation
- Workflow automation and custom fields
- Native integrations with 200+ tools
- Real-time collaboration and commenting
- Mobile apps (iOS and Android)

## Pricing
- **Free:** Limited features, up to 15 team members
- **Starter:** $13.49/user/month (billed annually)
- **Advanced:** $24.99/user/month
- **Enterprise:** Custom pricing for large organizations

## Strengths
1. **Mature Platform:** Established brand with strong market presence and extensive integrations ecosystem
2. **Advanced Planning:** Superior timeline/Gantt capabilities and resource management features
3. **Enterprise Ready:** Robust governance, security, and compliance features for large organizations

## Weaknesses
1. **Complexity:** Steep learning curve; requires time for team onboarding and configuration
2. **Pricing:** Higher per-user costs limit adoption for budget-conscious teams, especially remote startups
3. **Mobile Experience:** Mobile app lacks feature parity with desktop version

## Opportunities for TaskFlow
1. **Simplicity & Speed:** Focus on ease of use for distributed remote teams who need quick setup
2. **Transparent Pricing:** Fixed-price or per-team billing alternatives to per-user models
3. **Remote-First Design:** Purpose-built for asynchronous work and distributed team collaboration
